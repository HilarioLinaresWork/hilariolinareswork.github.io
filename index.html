Window Conversion & Folding Standing Workstation

I replaced a window that let in outside heat, noise and smoke, then framed and insulated the opening. I added wiring for two outlets and built a folding laptop shelf from glued plywood, hinged hardware and a reused support from an old TV stand.

VEHICLE CARE / DEEP CLEANING

Scion FR-S — Interior Disassembly & Odor Removal

I took my car interior apart after noticing an unpleasant smell. I removed seats, carpeting and trim, followed repair diagrams, and cleaned grease, dirt and hidden surfaces. Small plastic pieces were soaked in soap and water, larger ones scrubbed, and electrical areas cleaned with alcohol. A fan helped dry the carpeting before I reassembled everything.

Outcome: The unpleasant odor disappeared after I completed the deep cleaning and put the car interior back together.

COMPUTER MAINTENANCE

Dell 2-in-1 — Cooling Inspection & Reassembly

I opened my Dell 2-in-1 laptop to investigate excess heat, inspect its cooling fans and cables, and clean accessible internal areas. I kept small parts organized and took reference photos before carefully putting the components back together.

Outcome: Windows started successfully during a test before the final cover was installed. The laptop ran cooler afterward, although still warmer than I preferred.
